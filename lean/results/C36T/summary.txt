C36T: the c36t certificate (n = 36, lambda = 133/100) checked in Lean 4 with native_decide.

Theorems: Autocorr.C36T.autocorr_lower, autocorr_lower_ess, autocorr_lower_real (C_1 >= 133/100 in the pointwise-
supremum, essential-supremum and real-convolution forms).
Axioms (each theorem): propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound and 2,419,680 auxiliary axioms created by native_decide
(one per job of the certificate, plus one for the root box); no other axiom.
Input: the filled certificate (every leaf with the exact dual accepted by the verifier), 2,419,679 job files,
2,118.5 GB compressed; its sha256 list is filled_certificate.sha256.gz.
Modules: 490,435 shard modules in 202 shards (6,372 complete subtrees), one table module, 64 check modules for the 620
jobs at the top of the tree, and the top module Autocorr.C36T.Gro.Groot, which imports everything once.
Compute, for one build from the filled certificate: about 2,200 CPU-hours, of which about 1,300 compile the shard
modules (measured per module) and about 900 generate their sources (the exporter, about 0.018 CPU-seconds per leaf,
measured on a complete shard); on 202 servers with 96 to 384 threads this took about 25 minutes of wall time. The top
module took about 20 minutes on one server with 360 threads, most of it importing the 4.6 TB of compiled shard modules.
